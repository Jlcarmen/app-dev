Strong friendship and teamwork themes

Creepy, mysterious storyline

Great mix of sci-fi, horror, and adventure

📚 Short Summary

Stranger Things is set in the town of Hawkins, where kids uncover government secrets, supernatural threats, and a girl with extraordinary powers. Together, they face creatures from the “Upside Down” while trying to protect their town and each other.
